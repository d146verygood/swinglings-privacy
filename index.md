# Swinglings Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

Swinglings ("the game") is made by d146 ("we"). This page explains what information is collected
when you play.

## What we collect
We do not collect, store or sell any personal information ourselves. Your scores, coins, items and
settings are saved only on your device.

## Services the game uses
These services may collect information as described in their own privacy policies.

- **Google AdMob** (ads). It may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address (used for
  approximate location), how you use the app, and crash and performance data, to show ads and
  measure them. Players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised
  ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time with the **PRIVACY** button in the game.
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>
- **Google Play Billing** (purchases on Android) and **the Apple App Store** (purchases on iPhone and
  iPad). Payments are handled by Google Play or Apple. We never see your payment details. The game
  only receives a confirmation of what you bought, so it can give it to you.
  <https://policies.google.com/privacy> · <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>
- **Google Play Games Services** (leaderboards, optional). If you sign in, your Play Games profile
  name and your scores are shared with Google to show the leaderboards.
  <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

The game also asks www.google.com (or www.cloudflare.com if Google can't be reached) for the current
date and time, so daily rewards and seasonal events follow the real calendar. The request contains no
information about you or your device. As with any internet request, the server sees your IP address.
<https://policies.google.com/privacy> · <https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/>

On iPhone and iPad the game asks your permission before it uses the advertising identifier (App
Tracking Transparency). If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings >
Google > Ads), or on iPhone and iPad in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

## Children
The game is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
