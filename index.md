# Swinglings Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected and used when you use Swinglings (the
"Game"), developed by d146 ("we", "us" or "our"). By using the Game, you agree to the practices
described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect
We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. Your scores, in-game currency, items and
settings are stored locally on your device only.

## 2. Third-Party Services
The Game uses the following third-party services. Each service may collect information in accordance
with its own privacy policy.

**Google AdMob (advertising).** Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP
address (which may be used to determine approximate location), app interaction data and diagnostic
data in order to serve and measure advertisements. Users in the European Economic Area, the United
Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised advertisements are shown, and may
change their choice at any time through the PRIVACY button in the Game.
Privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>

**Google Play Billing and the Apple App Store (in-app purchases).** All payments are processed by
Google Play on Android devices and by Apple on iPhone and iPad. We do not receive or store your
payment information. The Game receives only a confirmation of the purchase in order to deliver the
purchased item.
Privacy policies: <https://policies.google.com/privacy> and <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

**Google Play Games Services (leaderboards, optional).** If you choose to sign in, your Play Games
profile name and scores are shared with Google in order to display leaderboards.
Privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## 3. Date and Time Requests
The Game requests the current date and time from www.google.com, or from www.cloudflare.com if Google
cannot be reached, so that daily rewards and seasonal events follow the actual calendar. These
requests do not include any information about you or your device. As with any internet request, the
receiving server can see your IP address.
Privacy policies: <https://policies.google.com/privacy> and <https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/>

## 4. Advertising Identifier and Your Choices
On iPhone and iPad, the Game requests your permission before accessing the advertising identifier, in
accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. If you decline, advertisements will still
be shown, but they will not be personalised.

You may reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalised advertising at any time:

- Android: Settings > Google > Ads
- iPhone and iPad: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking

## 5. Children's Privacy
The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 13.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page together
with a revised "Last updated" date.
