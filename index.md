# Privacy Policy — Swinglings

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

Swinglings ("the game") is developed by d146 ("we"). This policy explains what
information is collected when you play.

## Information we collect
We do **not** collect, store or sell any personal information ourselves. Your scores and
settings are stored only on your device.

## Third-party services
The game uses the following services, which may collect information as described in
their own privacy policies:

- **Google AdMob** (advertising) — may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address
  (used for approximate location), app interaction and diagnostic data to show and measure
  ads. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads
  are shown, and can change their choice anytime via the **PRIVACY** button in the game.
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>
- **Google Play Billing** (in-app purchases on Android) / **Apple App Store** (in-app purchases on
  iPhone and iPad) — payments are handled entirely by Google Play or Apple. We never see your
  payment details; the game only receives a confirmation of what was bought so it can deliver it.
  <https://policies.google.com/privacy> · <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>
- **Google Play Games Services** (leaderboards, optional) — if you sign in, your Play Games
  profile name and scores are shared with Google to show leaderboards.
  <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

The game also asks www.google.com (or www.cloudflare.com if Google can't be reached) for the
current date and time, so daily login rewards and seasonal events follow the real calendar.
This is a plain request with no data from you or your device; like any internet request, the
server sees your IP address. <https://policies.google.com/privacy> ·
<https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/>

On iPhone and iPad the game asks your permission before using the advertising identifier
(App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, you still get ads, just not personalised ones.

You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings
(Settings → Google → Ads), or on iPhone / iPad in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information
from them.

## Changes
We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
